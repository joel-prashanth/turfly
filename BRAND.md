# Turfly Brand Kit

Visual identity guidelines · 2025

---

## Logo

The wordmark is **Turfly** — the `T` is always rendered in Goal green. The remaining letters follow the background context (white on dark, Pitch black on light).

| Variant | Background | Wordmark color |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Pitch black `#090E09` | White + green T |
| Light | Chalk white `#F7F7F5` | Pitch black + green T |
| Neutral | White `#ffffff` | Pitch black + green T |

**Rules**
- The green T is the only colored element — never change it
- Do not rotate, skew, or apply effects to the wordmark
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without a dark overlay
- Minimum clear space = height of the T letterform on all sides

## Typography

### Display — Barlow Condensed 800

Used exclusively for hero headings. Always uppercase.

```
BOOK YOUR COURT.
PLAY AT THE CITY'S BEST TURFS.
```

> Font: Barlow Condensed · Weight: 800 · Case: UPPERCASE · Tracking: tight

### Subheadings — System sans 500

```
Real slots. Instant confirmation.
Three steps. Zero friction.
```

> Font: System sans-serif · Weight: 500 · Case: Sentence case

### Body — System sans 400

```
Find and reserve football, cricket, and badminton courts
across your city — no calls, no WhatsApp follow-ups.
```

> Font: System sans-serif · Weight: 400 · Line height: 1.7

> Font: System sans-serif · Weight: 700 · Case: UPPERCASE · Letter spacing: 0.15–0.2em · Color: Field green

## Voice and tone

Direct. Confident. No fluff. Sports culture — punchy, action-first. Every word earns its place.

### Do

- "Book your court."
- "Real slots. Instant confirmation."
- "No calls, no WhatsApp follow-ups."
- "Cancel up to 24h before."
- "3 results" not "3 results found"

### Don't

- "Experience seamless booking!"
- "Leverage our platform to unlock your potential."
- "Your booking was placed successfully!"
- "Please fill in the form below."
- Exclamation marks on system copy

### Principles

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Sentence case everywhere | "Save changes" not "Save Changes" |
| Verb-first CTAs | "View slots" not "Click to see slots" |
| No "successfully" | "Booking confirmed" not "Booking placed successfully" |
| No "please" | "Enter a name" not "Please enter a name" |
| Active voice | "Confirm instantly" not "Booking is confirmed instantly" |
| No filler words | Cut "simply", "just", "easy", "seamless", "unlock" |

## Usage rules

**Color**
Green text only on dark or white backgrounds. Minimum 12pt for Field green `#16a34a` on white. Never use Goal green `#22c55e` for body text.

**Spacing**
Generous whitespace. Section padding minimum 40px vertical. Card padding 16–20px.

**Photography**
Sports venues, action shots, night-lit turfs. Always apply a dark overlay (`rgba(0,0,0,0.25–0.5)`) when placing text over images.

**Backgrounds**
Preferred: Pitch black, Chalk white, photography with overlay. Avoid mid-grey backgrounds — they undercut the brand contrast.
